Standard CNNs are not invariant to these manipulations. Convolutions make a CNN equivariant to translations, but nothing in the architecture guarantees stable predictions when an image is flipped, zoomed, rotated, brightened, blurred or partly occluded. Any robustness is learned from the training data and its augmentations. The bars show the model's softmax outputs: they are model probabilities, not guarantees that a prediction is correct.
