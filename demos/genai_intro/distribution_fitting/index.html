Fidelity: generated samples in the target's 95% region
Coverage: training samples in the generator's 95% region
Average log-likelihood of the training data

The 95% region of a distribution is the smallest region that contains 95% of its probability, i.e. where its density is highest. A perfect generator reaches about 95% fidelity and coverage. Fidelity depends on the generated samples (number and seed); coverage and log-likelihood are computed on 1,000 fixed training samples drawn from the target. The fit by maximum likelihood also uses these training samples, never the generated ones.
